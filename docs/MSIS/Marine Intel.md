# Marine Intel

Bottom bar left side of [[Screen Layout]].

## Ocean Regions

Click a preset to fly there and set the Area of Interest (2x2 degree box).

- Arabian Sea (default)
- Bay of Bengal
- Indian Ocean
- South China Sea
- Atlantic Ocean
- Pacific Ocean

## Ocean Metrics

Generated by `oceanMetricsAt(lat, lng)` using deterministic hash of coordinates.

- SST — sea surface temperature
- Wind — speed and direction
- Sea State — Beaufort scale

## Historic Spills

- Exxon Valdez

- MV Rak Carrier
